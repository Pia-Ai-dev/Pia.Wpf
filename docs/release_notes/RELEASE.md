# Pia 1.4.82

## Routines editor

- Describe a routine in a sentence and let Pia write it. **Draft with AI**
  fills in the name, the instruction, the schedule and the tools the routine
  needs, in whatever language you wrote the description in. Nothing is saved
  until you have read it over.
- A drafted routine is only ever offered the tools this device actually has,
  and only the ones it names are ticked. Destructive tools Pia does not ship
  are never on offer, so a draft cannot grant one.
- Required fields are marked, and **Save** stays switched off until they are
  filled rather than refusing after the click. Name, Goal and Time are
  required — or, for a meeting routine, the Teams link and the recording
  acknowledgement in place of the goal.
- The editor pairs its dropdowns two to a row and gives the goal box room for
  the whole instruction, so a routine fits on one screen without scrolling.

## Routine blueprints

- Blueprints now open already written in German and French — the instruction,
  the example values and the field help, not just the title. The wording is
  fixed at the moment you create the routine, so changing the interface
  language later leaves your existing routines untouched.
- Blueprint instructions are around half as long: two or three sentences
  saying what to look at, what shape the answer takes, and how long it may be.
- A blueprint’s instruction shows the value you filled in — the topic, the
  teams, the markets — in colour, so you can see which words came from the
  field above it. Click the instruction to write your own wording instead.
